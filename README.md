# Some-Shared-Undergraduate-Resources-for-BNU-Physics-Majors

Course materials for the physics major at Beijing Normal University: homework, lab reports, lecture notes, slides, and exams. Names and student IDs in the lab reports have been replaced with 学生 and `[学号已脱敏]`.

Only physics-department courses are included. General-education courses such as English, political theory, psychology, and physical education are not here, nor are competitions, research projects, volunteer teaching, or full textbooks.

Each course uses only these subfolders, and a folder is omitted when the course has nothing of that kind: `homework`, `notes`, `slides`, `exams`, and `reports`. Assigned problems and answer keys live in `problems` and `solutions` under `homework`.
